Privacy Policy

Magnet Search ("Magnet Search", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the Magnet Search Android application and how that information is handled.

Magnet Search is designed as a client-side search and discovery application. It does not require you to create an account, and it does not operate a developer-owned server or proxy through which your searches are routed.

By using Magnet Search, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Effective Date: 14-09-2026 Last Updated: 14-09-2026

1. Who We Are

The data controller responsible for Magnet Search is:

Sruc Labs
sruclabs@gmail.com

For privacy questions or requests concerning information that we control, contact us at sruclabs@gmail.com.

2. Information We Do Not Require

Magnet Search does not require you to create an account.

We do not require or intentionally collect your:

  • name
  • email address
  • telephone number
  • physical address
  • password
  • account credentials
  • contacts
  • camera or microphone data
  • precise GPS location
  • files or documents from your device
  • biometric templates, fingerprints, facial data, PINs, or passwords

Magnet Search does not access or store the biometric information used by your device. Authentication for App Lock is performed by the Android operating system through its system authentication facilities.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Magnet Search stores certain information locally on your device so that the application can provide its features.

Search history

When Private Search is disabled, search queries and their submission times may be stored in the application's local database.

Search history remains on your device and is not uploaded by Magnet Search to a developer-operated server.

You can delete individual searches or clear your search history using the controls provided in the application.

Favorites

If you save a search result as a Favorite, Magnet Search may store information associated with that result, including:

  • the magnet URI
  • content title
  • BitTorrent information hash
  • formatted file size
  • source/provider name
  • the time the Favorite was saved

Favorites are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to a Magnet Search server.

Search suggestions

Magnet Search maintains a local query-suggestion cache to improve search convenience. The cache is limited in size and is maintained on the device.

Application settings

Magnet Search stores application settings locally, including applicable:

  • theme preferences
  • language preferences
  • enabled search sources
  • sorting preferences
  • Privacy Protection settings
  • App Lock settings
  • Auto-Lock preferences
  • Premium entitlement and related preference state

These settings are used to operate and personalize the application and are not uploaded to a Magnet Search server.

4. Searches and Third-Party Search Providers

When you perform a search, Magnet Search sends your search query directly to the search providers that are enabled for that search.

Magnet Search does not operate a search proxy or developer-owned server through which these queries are routed.

The production application may query the following sources:

  • Open Torrent Index, through its apibay.org search endpoint
  • Community Swarm Index, through its nyaa.si search endpoint
  • Internet Archive, through its archive.org search services
  • Research & Science Index, using the arXiv collection through the archive.org search service

The particular sources contacted depend on which sources are enabled in the application.

A search request may include your search terms and standard technical HTTP information, including the application's User-Agent.

Because the requests are made directly from your device to the relevant provider, that provider can also receive your public IP address and other network information that is ordinarily visible when an Internet connection is established.

Magnet Search does not add your name, email address, telephone number, account credentials, Android ID, or similar personal account identifier to these search requests.

Search providers operate independently of Magnet Search. Their own privacy policies, logging practices, retention periods, and legal obligations govern information they receive through their services. Magnet Search does not control or have access to their server logs and generally cannot delete information retained by those providers.

5. Search Results and Third-Party Content

Magnet Search retrieves search-result metadata from external providers and processes that information on the device.

Depending on the provider and result, this may include:

  • titles
  • content identifiers
  • BitTorrent information hashes
  • file sizes
  • categories
  • dates
  • seed and peer counts
  • descriptions or other metadata
  • magnet-related information

Search-result information is third-party content supplied by external providers. Magnet Search may normalize or transform this information for display, including generating a magnet URI from available metadata.

Search-result information is generally held temporarily in application memory. If you explicitly save a result as a Favorite, relevant information is stored locally on your device.

6. Opening Magnet Links in Other Applications

If you choose to open a magnet link, Magnet Search uses the Android operating system's standard application handoff mechanism to pass the selected magnet URI to a compatible application installed on your device.

The receiving application may be a BitTorrent or other magnet-compatible client.

Once the magnet URI has been handed to another application, that application's own privacy policy, data practices, network connections, and retention practices apply.

Magnet Search does not control what a third-party application does with a magnet URI after you choose to open it.

7. Advertising and Google AdMob

Free users may be shown advertisements through Google AdMob.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information necessary to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services. Depending on the applicable configuration, consent status, device settings, and applicable law, this may include:

  • IP address
  • advertising identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID
  • applicable device or app identifiers
  • device and technical information
  • operating-system and application information
  • ad impressions and interactions
  • information concerning advertising performance
  • diagnostic and performance information
  • information used to detect fraud, abuse, or invalid advertising activity

Some information may be used to determine general or approximate location from network information, such as an IP address, where applicable to advertising services. Magnet Search does not request or access your precise GPS location for advertising.

Advertising data is transmitted directly between the application and Google's advertising infrastructure and applicable advertising partners. Magnet Search does not operate an advertising server or proxy.

Google's processing of information through its advertising services is governed by Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

The specific advertising technologies and processing that apply to you can depend on your location, consent choices, device settings, and the configuration of the advertising service.

Where required, Magnet Search uses Google's consent-management mechanisms to obtain and manage advertising consent.

8. Advertising Consent and Google UMP

Magnet Search uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) as part of its advertising consent framework.

Where consent is legally required, UMP may present privacy and consent choices relating to advertising and associated data processing.

Your choices may include whether certain forms of personalized advertising or related processing are permitted.

Consent information may be stored locally by the UMP SDK using standardized consent mechanisms and may be communicated to relevant advertising systems so that your choices can be respected.

Where applicable, Magnet Search provides a way for you to review or change your advertising privacy choices.

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other jurisdictions where applicable, advertising consent is handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements and Google's applicable consent requirements. Google specifically requires appropriate consent mechanisms and the ability to withdraw consent where applicable.

Changing or withdrawing advertising consent may affect the type of advertising you receive, including whether personalized advertising is available.

9. Premium and Advertising

Premium is intended to provide an ad-free experience.

When Premium is active, Magnet Search is designed not to request or display advertising through the application's normal advertising workflow.

The final implementation will ensure that advertising requests are appropriately suppressed for Premium users rather than merely hiding an unused advertising container.

11. Privacy Protection

Magnet Search includes optional Privacy Protection features designed to protect information displayed or stored within the application on your device.

Depending on your Premium status and settings, these features may include:

  • App Lock
  • Auto-Lock
  • Private Search
  • Hide Recent Searches
  • Protect Favorites
  • Screenshot Protection

App Lock

App Lock uses Android's system authentication mechanisms, including biometric authentication and device credentials where supported.

Magnet Search does not access or store your fingerprint, face data, biometric templates, PIN, pattern, or password.

The Android operating system handles the underlying authentication.

Private Search

When Private Search is enabled, newly submitted searches are prevented from being added to Magnet Search's local search history.

Private Search does not prevent the search query from being transmitted to the external search providers required to perform the search.

Hide Recent Searches

This feature controls whether existing recent searches are displayed in the application's search interface. It does not delete the underlying history unless the applicable deletion function is used.

Protect Favorites

This feature requires device authentication before the contents of the Favorites area are displayed.

Screenshot Protection

When enabled, Magnet Search uses Android's secure-window functionality to restrict screenshots, screen recording, and application previews in the system's recent-apps interface.

Important Privacy Protection Limitation

Privacy Protection is an on-device application security feature.

It is not a VPN, does not anonymize Internet traffic, does not hide your IP address, and does not prevent external search providers from observing network information associated with requests made to their services.
