Privacy Policy

Magnet Search ("Magnet Search", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the Magnet Search Android application and how that information is handled.

Magnet Search is designed as a client-side search and discovery application. It does not require you to create an account, and it does not operate a developer-owned server or proxy through which your searches are routed.

By using Magnet Search, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Effective date: 14-09-2026.

Last updated: 14-09-2026.

The English version of this policy is the authoritative source.

Information We Store

Magnet Search does not require you to create an account, and it does not intentionally collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, files, or biometric data. Authentication for App Lock is performed by the Android operating system.

To provide its features, Magnet Search stores certain information locally on your device, including:

  • Search history (when Private Search is disabled), with submission times — deletable individually or cleared entirely
  • Saved Favorites (magnet URI, title, info hash, size, source name, saved time)
  • A limited local query-suggestion cache that trims itself automatically
  • App settings (theme, language, enabled sources, sorting, Privacy Protection, App Lock, Auto-Lock, Premium state)

This information stays on your device and is not uploaded to a developer-operated server. The app protects it with Android sandboxing, encrypted HTTPS connections to external services, and system authentication for App Lock.

Searches

When you perform a search, Magnet Search sends your query directly to the search providers enabled for that search. There is no developer-owned search proxy or server in between.

Depending on your enabled sources, the app may query:

  • Internet Archive
  • Research & Science Index

A request includes your search terms and standard technical HTTP information such as the app's User-Agent. Because requests go directly from your device, a provider can also see your public IP address and other ordinary network information, which may be processed in other countries under that provider's own policies.

Magnet Search never adds your name, email, credentials, Android ID, or similar identifiers to search requests. Providers operate independently: their own privacy policies, logging, and retention govern what they receive, and Magnet Search cannot access or delete their server logs.

Accounts

Magnet Search does not require you to create an account or provide an email address, password, or other credentials.

We do not require or intentionally collect your name, telephone number, physical address, contacts, camera or microphone data, precise GPS location, device files, IMEI or similar hardware identifiers, Android ID, or browsing history outside Magnet Search. Biometric authentication for App Lock is handled entirely by Android; the app never accesses or stores biometric templates, PINs, or passwords.

The app is not intended to collect personal information from children and does not intentionally request names, email addresses, telephone numbers, or similar personal information from users.

Tracking

Magnet Search itself runs no tracking server, advertising server, remote analytics, or crash-reporting service, and uses no developer-operated cookies.

Free users may see ads served through Google AdMob. Google's ads SDK may process your IP address, advertising ID, device and OS information, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic data to provide, measure, and secure ads, and may derive approximate (never precise GPS) location from network information. Ads data flows directly between the app and Google's infrastructure.

Where required by law, consent is managed through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP); you can review or change your advertising choices where offered, including in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland. Withdrawing consent may change whether personalized ads are shown.

Premium is intended to be ad-free: when Premium is active, the app is designed not to request or display ads.

The optional Privacy Protection features (App Lock, Auto-Lock, Private Search, Hide Recent Searches, Protect Favorites, Screenshot Protection) guard information inside the app on your device. Note that Private Search only stops local history recording — the query is still sent to providers — and Privacy Protection is not a VPN: it does not hide your IP address or anonymize traffic.

Third-Party Apps

Search-result metadata (titles, identifiers, info hashes, sizes, categories, dates, seed and peer counts, descriptions, magnet information) comes from external providers and is processed on your device; the app may normalize it, including generating magnet URIs. Results live temporarily in memory unless you save a Favorite.

When you choose to open a magnet link, Magnet Search passes that link to a compatible app installed on your device via Android's standard handoff. Once you leave Magnet Search and use another app, that app's own privacy practices and policies apply.

Premium purchases are not yet available and will be offered through Google Play in a future update. When launched, Google Play will handle payment instruments, taxes, and refunds; Magnet Search never receives or stores card or bank details, and may only process purchase identifiers or subscription status supplied by Google Play to determine your entitlement.

Independent third-party services are governed by their own policies, including Google and Google Mobile Ads / AdMob, Google UMP, Google Play, Internet Archive, and Research & Science Index.

Data Removal

Local app information is kept until you delete it, change the setting, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Search history can be deleted individually or cleared entirely; Favorites can be removed individually; the suggestion cache trims itself automatically when it reaches its size limit; settings persist until changed or cleared. The local database holding your history, Favorites, and suggestions is excluded from Google cloud backup and device transfers, so uninstalling the app removes it.

Magnet Search does not control how long external search providers, Google AdMob, Google UMP, Google Play, or third-party apps retain information they process — their own policies and legal obligations govern retention.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality, third-party services, legal requirements, or data practices change. Material changes update the "Last Updated" date and, where appropriate, receive additional notice in the app or another suitable channel. The current version is always available on the official Magnet Search website and within the app.

Contact

The data controller for Magnet Search is Sruc Labs — contact sruclabs@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests about information we control.

Where laws like the GDPR apply, processing may rely on providing the service you requested, your consent (for example personalized ads via UMP), legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, service integrity), or legal obligations; third-party services act under their own responsibilities. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to be informed, access, correct, delete, restrict, object, data portability, withdraw consent, or complain to a supervisory authority. Because Magnet Search keeps no accounts or central user database, most app data lives only on your device and can be removed with the in-app controls, Android app-data controls, or uninstall; for data held by Google or providers, contact them directly.
