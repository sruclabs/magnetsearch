Privacy Policy

Magnet Search is designed to keep your searches and preferences on your device wherever possible.

Information We Store

Magnet Search stores certain information locally on your device, including:

  • Recent search history
  • Your Favorites
  • App preferences, such as your selected theme and enabled search providers

This information is stored locally and is not used to create an account or profile.

Searches

When you perform a search, your search request is sent directly to the search providers used by the app.

The app does not use its own server or proxy to process your searches.

Accounts

Magnet Search does not require you to create an account or provide an email address, password, or other account credentials to use the app.

Tracking

Magnet Search does not use a separate tracking server for your searches or require an account-based tracking system.

Your search history and app preferences are stored locally on your device.

Third-Party Apps

When you choose to open a magnet link, Magnet Search passes that link to a compatible app installed on your device.

Once you leave Magnet Search and use another app, that app's own privacy practices and policies apply.

Data Removal

You can remove your saved Favorites and search history from within the app where those controls are provided.

Uninstalling the app also removes its locally stored app data according to your device's normal Android app-data behavior.

Changes to This Policy

If the privacy practices of Magnet Search change, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For questions or concerns about Magnet Search, use the support/contact option provided with the app or its store listing.
