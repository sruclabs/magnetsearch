Built-in Security

On-Device Privacy Protection

Magnet Search never saves your queries to any server. You can also turn on on-device privacy settings to lock the app and keep your searches private.

Direct Connection: Your phone talks directly to the sources you pick. There's no proxy server reading your queries in between.
No Account Needed: You don't have to create an account, enter an email, or sign in to use the app.
Privacy Protection keeps your searches and saved links safe on your phone. It doesn't hide your internet traffic from your ISP or act as a VPN.

Privacy Settings

  • App Lock: Unlock the app using your fingerprint, face unlock, or device PIN
  • Private Search Mode: Stops recent searches from being saved to your device
  • Hide Recent Searches: Hides past search suggestions from under the search bar
  • Protect Saved Favorites: Requires fingerprint or PIN verification before showing your bookmarked links
  • Block Screenshots: Prevents screenshots and screen recordings while using the app
